V6 UPDATE METHOD
================
1. Keep the existing keywords.csv and posted_log.json in GitHub.
2. Delete all other old files and folders from the repository.
3. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
4. Upload everything inside the extracted folder.
5. When keywords.csv or posted_log.json conflict, keep your existing GitHub versions.
6. Confirm this exact path exists: .github/workflows/auto-post.yml
7. Open Actions > Facebook Auto Poster v6 Verified Publishing > Run workflow.
8. Check logs/last-publish-status.json and the clean permalink in Actions logs.
